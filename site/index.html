Under the Hood

Built with Cutting-Edge Client-Side AI

Kites eliminates heavy Python dependencies and expensive cloud servers by running optimized neural networks directly in Chromium via WebGPU.

⚡

WebGPU Hardware Acceleration

Runs ONNX Runtime Web directly on your graphics card with seamless WASM fallback. No local CUDA or Python installation needed.

📐

Kruskal-MST Spatial Clustering

Reconstructs reading order and merges fragmented text lines into unified dialogue balloons using graph-theoretic minimum spanning trees.

🎨

Dynamic 64px Patch Inpainting

LaMa Manga and AOT-GAN neural inpainters operate on localized dynamic-bucketed crops at 1:1 scale, avoiding ugly gray blur boxes.

🔀

Smart Translation Waterfall

Automatic failover across on-device WebLLM, our zero-cost Cloudflare community pool, Google Translate, or your private API keys.

🔒

100% Private & Local-First

Your images never touch our servers. All OCR detections, clean backgrounds, and translations are stored locally in IndexedDB with 7-day TTL pruning.

📖

100% Free & Open Source

Licensed under GPLv3. Auditable source code, zero hidden analytics, zero telemetry beacons, and zero monthly subscriptions.
